    Quick Reference

    SONG
    The song's structure on the screen — click a pattern to edit it (it loops while playing), click a chain chip to play the song from there. ＋ adds, ⧉ duplicates.
    M
    Mute a lane (silences it; FX state preserved)
    S
    Solo a lane (silences all others)
    VIEW
    Open that lane in the step editor / piano roll
    FILLS
    Queue fill patterns — click a chip to remove it from the queue
    PUNCH
    Hold a pad (or keys 1–5) for momentary FX — release to snap back clean
    TEMPO
    Song speed in BPM — on MASTER
    TRANSPOSE ±
    Shift the whole song up or down in semitones — on MASTER
    VOL
    How loud this instrument is
    PAN
    Push the sound towards the left or right speaker
    BAL
    Lean the whole mix left or right
    WID
    How wide the sound feels — narrow mono to extra-wide stereo
    CUT
    Brightness — left for dark and muffled, right for thin and crispy, middle is natural
    RES
    Adds a whistly squelch right where the brightness sits — great for acid bass
    DRV
    Crunch — pushes the sound like a guitar amp turned up too far
    LO
    More or less bass, for everything at once
    HI
    More or less sparkle, for everything at once
    DLY
    Echo — how much of the sound bounces back
    FDBK
    How long the echoes keep going — one quick slap to a long trailing wash
    CHO
    Shimmer — makes the sound thicker and dreamier
    WOB
    Makes the sound wobble in slow waves
    CRU
    Crunchy retro lo-fi, like an old game console
    VRB
    Room sound — from a small box to a huge hall
    CMP
    Squashes loud and quiet closer together so everything punches
