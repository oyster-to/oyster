    Groovebox — Quick Reference

    Mix

    Volume
    Lane output level
    Pan
    Left / right placement
    Balance
    Master left / right
    Width
    Stereo width (mono ↔ wide)

    Tone

    Cutoff
    Filter sweep — left = low-pass (darker), right = high-pass (thinner), centre = open
    Resonance
    Emphasis at the filter cutoff (squelch)
    Drive
    Overdrive / distortion
    Low EQ
    Master bass shelf
    High EQ
    Master treble shelf

    FX

    Delay
    Echo send amount
    Feedback
    How many times the delay repeats (slap → wash)
    Chorus
    Shimmer / width
    Wobble
    LFO auto-filter movement
    Crush
    Bit-crusher (lo-fi)
    Reverb
    Space / room send
    Comp
    Compression — evens out dynamics

    Controls

    M
    Mute a lane (silences it; FX state preserved)
    S
    Solo a lane (silences all others)
    VIEW
    Open that lane in the step editor / piano roll
    FILLS
    Queue fill patterns — click a chip to remove it from the queue
    SONG tab
    The song's structure on the screen — click a pattern to edit it (it loops while playing), click a chain chip to play the song from there. ＋ adds, ⧉ duplicates.
    PUNCH
    Hold a pad (or keys 1–5) for momentary FX — release to snap back clean
    TEMPO
    Song speed in BPM — on MASTER
    TRANSPOSE ±
    Shift the whole song up or down in semitones — on MASTER
